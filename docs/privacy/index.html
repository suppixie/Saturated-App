Privacy & GDPR Notice

Effective: 7 August 2026 · Last updated: 11 August 2026

This notice explains how Saturated handles personal data and how you can exercise your rights.

1. Who controls your data

The controller is , operating the Saturated app from Ireland. Contact: . The supervisory authority in Ireland is the Data Protection Commission.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: email address, display name, username, authentication provider and account identifiers.
  • Age assurance: date of birth and the date age eligibility was confirmed. We use this to enforce the minimum service age.
  • Profile: optional photo, biography and social connections.
  • Activity: reviews, ratings, flavour tags, comments, likes, Drinklist entries, follows, badges and drink requests.
  • Safety: reports, blocks, moderation decisions and related audit records.
  • Technical: security, authentication, error and service logs generated by our providers; an app-generated random identifier; app version and platform; screen names; feature-event counts; and sanitised crash messages and stack traces. Analytics do not include review or comment text, email addresses, access tokens, precise GPS location, or search terms.

3. Why we process data

Purpose Typical legal basis under GDPR
Create and operate accounts; provide reviews, lists, follows and badges Performance of our contract with you
Age eligibility and service safety Legitimate interests and compliance with applicable obligations
Security, abuse prevention, reports, blocking and moderation Legitimate interests; legal obligation where applicable
Optional profile image and optional communications Consent, which can be withdrawn
Privacy-minimised product analytics, service diagnostics and reliability Legitimate interests in operating a secure and reliable service

4. Public information

Your display name, username, profile image, reviews, ratings, flavour tags, comments, likes and buddy relationships may be visible to other users. Your email address and date of birth are not public. Blocking hides affected profiles and their content from each other.

5. Service providers and transfers

We use Supabase for authentication, database and file storage; Expo/EAS for app build and delivery infrastructure; Apple and Google when you choose their sign-in services; and GitHub Pages to host these public policies. These providers process data under their own terms and data-protection arrangements. Some processing may involve transfers outside the EEA using recognised transfer safeguards where required.

6. Retention

Account data is normally retained while your account remains active. User content is retained until you delete it, delete your account, or it is removed through moderation. Safety and audit records may be retained for up to 24 months where necessary to prevent repeated abuse, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. Residual copies may remain temporarily in secured provider backups until those backups rotate. Product analytics and sanitised crash reports are normally retained for up to 90 days, then deleted or aggregated so they no longer identify an account or installation.

7. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and withdrawal of consent. You can export key account data and delete your account inside Settings → GDPR regulations. You can also contact us. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Saturated’s recommendation ordering and badges do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

8. Account deletion

Deleting your account removes the active profile and associated reviews, comments, likes, Drinklist entries, follows and requests. Follow the steps on our account deletion page.

9. Security and breaches

We use access controls, row-level database security, encrypted network connections, provider authentication and restricted moderator access. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Where required, qualifying personal-data breaches will be reported to the relevant authority and affected individuals.

10. Adults only

Saturated is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account, contact us so it can be investigated.

11. Changes

We may update this notice as the service changes. Material changes will be communicated in the app or through another appropriate channel.
