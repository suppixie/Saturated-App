Support

For account, privacy, safety or catalogue help, email or visit @saturated.app.

Sign-in and password help

Use “Forgot password?” on the email sign-in screen. Open the secure reset link on the same phone, choose a new password, then return to Saturated. Google or Apple users should also check that the email associated with that provider is accessible.

Email confirmation

New email accounts must open the confirmation link before signing in. Check spam or junk folders and make sure the address was entered correctly.

Report safety issues

Use Report on a user, review or comment. Use Block user for immediate separation. For urgent safety or rights concerns, email support with the username, content and relevant context.

Privacy requests

Settings → GDPR regulations provides data export and permanent account deletion. For access, correction, restriction, objection or portability questions, contact the privacy address above.

Catalogue corrections

Send the drink name, brand, correct information and an authoritative source. Rights owners may also request image or catalogue corrections.

Response targets

We prioritise safety and account-access issues. We aim to acknowledge ordinary support requests within three business days and urgent content reports as quickly as reasonably possible.
