Community Guidelines

Effective: 7 August 2026

Share honest beverage experiences, respect other people, and help keep discovery useful and safe.

Be genuine

  • Review drinks you have actually tried.
  • Disclose material relationships with brands or venues.
  • Do not coordinate fake ratings, spam, scams or deceptive promotions.

Respect people

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, stalking, doxxing or unwanted sexual conduct.
  • No hate speech or attacks based on protected characteristics.
  • No impersonation or sharing another person’s private information.

Keep content appropriate

  • No sexual exploitation, graphic sexual content, violence or self-harm encouragement.
  • No illegal sales, instructions for criminal activity, or promotion of dangerous substance use.
  • No dangerous drinking challenges, drink-driving encouragement or pressure to consume alcohol.

Respect rights

  • Only upload text and images you created or are authorised to use.
  • Do not misuse trademarks or pretend to represent a beverage brand.
  • Send copyright and trademark concerns through Support.

Reporting and blocking

Use the clearly labelled Report control on profiles, reviews and comments. Choose the closest reason and add useful context. Use Block user when you no longer want to see or interact with an account. Reports are private; false or abusive reporting may itself lead to action.

What moderators may do

Moderators may dismiss a report, hide content, suspend or reinstate an account, and preserve an audit record. Serious or repeated violations can lead to permanent account removal and, where necessary, referral to appropriate authorities.

Appeals and urgent concerns

To appeal a moderation action, contact . If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services rather than relying on an in-app report.
