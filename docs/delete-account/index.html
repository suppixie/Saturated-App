Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Saturated account and associated active data from inside the app.

Delete inside Saturated

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select GDPR regulations.
  4. Select Delete account permanently.
  5. Confirm the deletion warning.

This cannot be undone. Your active profile, reviews, comments, likes, Drinklist, follows, requests, blocks and reports are deleted. Content already removed from public view will not be restored.

If you cannot access the app

Email from the address associated with your account with the subject “Saturated account deletion”. We will verify ownership before acting. Never send your password.

Retention after deletion

Deletion removes active account data promptly. Limited security/audit information and temporary encrypted backup copies may remain where legally necessary or until provider backups rotate, as described in the Privacy Notice.
